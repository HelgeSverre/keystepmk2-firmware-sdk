KEYSTEP MK2000

Four tracks, an arpeggiator, chords, scales and chains for the 32-key KeyStep mk2. Unofficial firmware.

Everything you do while playing is a held button plus a key. The keys keep the printing they have, and this guide names them by it: four bank keys, sixteen numbered keys, and twelve edit keys (the ones printed Rate and Seq Edit). The words in blue under a button are what it does with Shift.

Hold a button: what the keys do

Click a button on the drawing, or pick one here. Let go and the keys play again; a release only counts as a tap within a third of a second, so holding to look costs nothing.

Your first pattern

Record

Stopped, press Record. The display reads T1 Rec.

Enter the steps

A note or chord for each step. Hold adds a rest; keys + Hold ties into the next step.

Finish and play

Record again: the track is as long as what you entered. Then Play.

Add a track

Seq + 2 selects track 2. Record it the same way; it can be a different length.

Save

Shift + Seq, then click. Nothing is saved by itself.

The step page

Seq + turn opens it for the selected track.

A four-step track, on step 2. Filled cell: notes. Outline: a rest. The bar marks the last step that plays.

Past the end. Steps after the track's length are dots, a small block when they hold a note. They are silent until the length reaches them.

turn
Along the steps, on past the end, up to 64.
keys
Put that note on the step. Held together: a chord. You hear it; the page stays on the step.
Hold
The step becomes a rest. Shift + Undo brings it back.
click
The step's fields: Velocity, Gate, Probability, Ratchet 1–4, Condition, Tie, Offset.
Seq + numbered key
Go to that step of the 16 shown.
Seq + turn
Move 16 steps.
Seq + bank key
Another track.
Back
Out of the fields, then out of the page.
A note entered past the end never changes the length. Set the length with Shift + Tempo: prepare the second half of a phrase out of sight, then lengthen the track to bring it in.

Strips and pedal

Bend strip
Pitch bend. Springs back to the centre.

Mod wheel, CC 1. Stays where you leave it.
Pedal
Sustain, CC 64.
Aftertouch
Channel pressure.

Sync and MIDI

Clock Receive: Auto uses its own clock until one arrives on USB or MIDI, follows it, and returns two seconds after it stops. MK2000 always sends clock and the sync output.

MIDI Thru passes the MIDI input on to the MIDI output and USB. USB to MIDI passes USB on to the MIDI output.

Memory

MK2000 keeps its patterns where Arturia's firmware never looks, and never touches Arturia's patterns, settings or calibration. Switch between the two as often as you like: each finds its own as it left them.
